# Knee Slice Leg Weave Pass to Submission

## Overview
A guard passing to submission drill where the top player starts in knee slice position, commits to a knee slice, leg weave, or X pass, then must advance into side control or mount and finish with a submission. The bottom player defends the pass and looks to sweep, submit, or take the back.

## Details
**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

### Top Player
**Position:** Upright in knee slice position with your shin across bottom player's thigh, outside leg posted, while bottom player has a knee shield established  
**Objective:** Break down the knee shield, complete a knee slice, leg weave, or X pass to side control or mount before attacking, then isolate an arm and finish a submission  
**Win:** Successfully submit the bottom player after completing the pass

### Bottom Player
**Position:** On your back and side with knee shield established against top player who is in knee slice position  
**Objective:** Defend the pass, create offensive reactions, and attack immediately when the top player commits weight  
**Win:** Successfully sweep and come on top, submit the top player, or take the back with control

## Instructions
1. Top player starts upright in knee slice position with shin across bottom player's thigh and outside leg posted
2. Bottom player starts with knee shield established and active frames
3. Top player breaks down the knee shield and commits to a knee slice, leg weave, or X pass
4. After passing to side control or mount, top player works to isolate an arm and finish a submission
5. Bottom player defends the pass and can win by sweeping, submitting, or taking the back at any point
6. If top player passes but loses dominant control before submitting, continue until either player achieves a win condition
7. Reset after a submission, sweep, or back take
8. Switch roles after the predetermined time

## Coaching Points
For top player:
- Commit fully to the pass once the knee shield begins to collapse
- Use the pass to create chest pressure before hunting the arm
- From side control, isolate the near arm with wrist and elbow control before setting figure four attacks
- From mount, keep your weight connected while moving toward s-mount or armbar finishing positions
- Do not rush the submission before the pass is stable
- Follow defensive turns into back exposure or mount when the bottom player over-rotates

For bottom player:
- Use the knee shield and frames to force the passer's weight onto their hands
- Attack sweeps as the top player commits to one passing lane
- Look for triangles, omoplatas, kimuras, or back takes when the top player reaches for upper body control
- If the pass is completed, immediately frame, turn, and create an angle before the submission grips settle
- Keep elbows tight when the top player starts isolating arms from side control or mount
