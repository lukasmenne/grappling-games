# Quarter Guard Underhook Progression Passing

## Overview
A progressive quarter guard passing game where the top player can start each reset from either double underhooks or single underhook and work to complete the pass.

## Details
**Time:** 3 minutes per role, 1 partner or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

### Top Player
**Position:** Top of quarter guard with the option to start each reset from either (1) double underhooks with chest-to-chest pressure, or (2) single underhook with the bottom player on their side  
**Objective:** Maintain upper-body control, flatten your partner when needed, and free your trapped leg to complete the pass  
**Win:** Pass to side control with chest connection for 3 seconds, or establish mount for 3 seconds

### Bottom Player
**Position:** Bottom of quarter guard while defending either double underhook or single underhook control  
**Objective:** Clear underhook control, recover strong frames, and insert knees/elbows to recover guard or stand  
**Win:** Remove underhook control and recover any form of guard, or stand up with separation

## Instructions
1. Start in quarter guard with top player choosing either double underhooks or single underhook
2. Top player works to maintain control, flatten when needed, and free the trapped leg to pass
3. Bottom player works to clear control and recover guard structure or stand
4. After each reset, top player may choose either start option again
5. Switch roles at halfway point

## Coaching Points
For top player:
- Prioritize chest-to-chest pressure before trying to free the trapped leg
- Keep your hips heavy and your head positioned to limit bottom player movement
- Use your free leg actively to pummel and extract the trapped knee line

For bottom player:
- Fight for inside elbow position early to prevent deep control
- Build frames before trying to move your hips away
- If flattened, recover angle first, then reinsert knee or shin as a frame
