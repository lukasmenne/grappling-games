# Spider Guard Foot Pin to Covered Hips Pass

## Overview
A spider guard passing drill where the top player converts broken spider guard connections into a full pass with the bottom player's hips covered. The top player works to pass to chest-to-chest with a closed loop around the head and arm, or chest-to-back with a seatbelt grip or double underhooks and feet covering the bottom player's hips.

## Details
**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

### Bottom Player
**Position:** On your back with sleeve grips and feet on your partner's biceps (spider guard)  
**Objective:** Use spider guard connections to sweep, submit, recover guard, or get underneath your partner and connect to both legs  
**Win:** Successfully sweep your partner, submit them, or establish connection to both of their legs with your hands and feet  

### Top Player
**Position:** On your knees or standing with your partner in spider guard  
**Objective:** Break the spider guard connections, pin one or both feet, and fully pass to chest-to-chest with a closed loop around their head and arm or chest-to-back and cover the bottom player's hips with a seatbelt grip or double underhooks  
**Win:** Pass to chest-to-chest with a closed loop around their head and arm or chest-to-back and cover the bottom player's hips with a seatbelt grip or double underhooks for 3 seconds  

## Instructions
1. Partners start with the bottom player on their back in spider guard with sleeve grips and feet on biceps
2. Bottom player works to sweep, submit, recover guard, or get underneath to connect to both legs
3. Top player works to break spider guard connections and pin one or both feet to the mat
4. Top player must convert the foot pin into a full pass to chest-to-chest with a closed loop around the head and arm, or chest-to-back with the hips covered using a seatbelt grip or double underhooks
5. When either player achieves their win condition, reset and switch roles

## Coaching Points
For bottom players:
- Keep your sleeve grips active and use your hips to create angles before your feet are pinned
- Recover guard immediately when the top player starts clearing your legs
- Look for sweeps, submissions, or leg connections when the top player overcommits to the pass

For top players:
- Break one spider guard connection at a time before trying to pass
- Keep the pinned foot controlled until your chest connection or hip coverage is established
- Cover the hips before settling so the bottom player cannot easily re-guard
- Choose chest-to-chest when you can close the loop around the head and arm, and chest-to-back when the bottom player turns away
