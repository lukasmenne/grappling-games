# Closed Guard to Feet on Hips - Upper Body Attacks

## Overview
A guard drill starting from closed guard where the guard player opens their guard to place both feet on the top player's hips while controlling both sleeves. The guard player works to off-balance and attack with triangles, armbars, kimuras, and omoplatas or sweep, while the top player works to clear the feet and grips and pass to a connected chest-to-chest or chest-to-back position.

## Details
**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

### Guard Player
**Position:** On your back starting in closed guard, then opening your guard to put both feet on your partner's hips with a grip on each of their sleeves  
**Objective:** Use your feet and sleeve grips to off-balance your partner and attack with a triangle, armbar, kimura, or omoplata, or sweep to the top position  
**Win:** Successfully lock up or finish a triangle, armbar, kimura, or omoplata (depending on comfortability), or sweep and come on top

### Top Player
**Position:** Kneeling inside your partner's guard with both of their feet on your hips and both of your sleeves gripped  
**Objective:** Strip the sleeve grips, clear the feet off your hips, and pass the guard  
**Win:** Successfully pass the guard and establish chest-to-chest or chest-to-back contact with your hands connected

## Instructions
1. Both players start with the guard player in closed guard and the top player kneeling inside the guard
2. Guard player establishes a grip on each sleeve, then opens the guard and places both feet on the top player's hips
3. Guard player works to off-balance the top player and create angles for submissions or sweeps
4. Top player works to strip the grips and clear the feet without getting swept or submitted
5. If the guard player loses a grip or a foot placement, they work to recover it or transition to another attack
6. The round ends when either player achieves their win condition
7. Reset and repeat, switching roles after the predetermined time

## Coaching Points
For the guard player:
- Keep your feet on the hips with your knees bent so you have room to extend and off-balance
- Pull one sleeve across your body to break your partner's posture and expose the far arm
- When your partner pushes into you, use their pressure to load them up for the sweep
- When they pull back, follow them and attack the arm with the armbar or kimura
- Drop the leg on the sleeve-controlled side to the omoplata or swing it up for the triangle when their arm crosses your centerline
- Keep angles - attacking straight on makes every submission harder to finish
- Do not let both grips get stripped at once; fight to keep at least one connection

For the top player:
- Keep your posture stacked over your hips so the feet cannot fold you forward
- Strip one grip at a time and immediately pin or pass the hand so it does not get re-gripped
- Drive your hips forward to pressure the guard player's legs rather than pulling back away from them
- Watch your elbows - a loose elbow is what feeds the kimura, armbar, and omoplata
- Once you clear the feet, close the distance immediately and connect your hands before the guard player recovers
