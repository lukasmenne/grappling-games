# Standing to Back Control Progression Class
**Class Length:** 1 hour

This class builds a continuous top-game sequence from standing exchanges through takedowns, guard passing, and pinning progressions, finishing with back control escapes and attacks. Students will work through six drills that connect these positions, practicing both the offensive path to back control and the defensive responses at each stage.

This class consists of several progressive drills that build upon each other. Each drill is available as an individual game file for reference.

1. [Knee Touch and Mat Touch](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/standing/knee-touch-and-mat-touch.md)
2. [Single Leg Takedown Variations](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/standing/single-leg-takedown-variations.md)
3. [HQ Position Passing Drill](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/guard/supine/hq-position-passing-drill.md)
4. [Side Control Framing and Pinning](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/pinning/side-control-framing-and-pinning.md)
5. [Progressive Mount Wrist Control to Isolation](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/pinning/mount/progressive-mount-wrist-control-to-isolation.md)
6. [Back Control Any Attack](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/pinning/back-control-any-attack.md)

**Total Drilling Time:** 36 minutes
- Standing Drills: 18 minutes
  - Knee Touch and Mat Touch: 6 minutes (2 x 3-minute rounds)
  - Single Leg Takedown Variations: 6 minutes (2 x 3-minute rounds)
- Guard Drills: 6 minutes
  - HQ Position Passing Drill: 6 minutes (2 x 3-minute rounds)
- Pinning Drills: 18 minutes
  - Side Control Framing and Pinning: 6 minutes (2 x 3-minute rounds)
  - Progressive Mount Wrist Control to Isolation: 6 minutes (2 x 3-minute rounds)
  - Back Control Any Attack: 6 minutes (2 x 3-minute rounds)

## Standing Drills

### 1.1 - [Knee Touch and Mat Touch](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/standing/knee-touch-and-mat-touch.md)

**Overview:** A competitive standing drill where both partners race to touch behind their opponent's knee or force their opponent's hands to touch the mat

**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

**Position:** Standing facing your partner with no initial connections  
**Objective:** Touch behind your partner's knee or get their hands to touch the mat  
**Win:** Successfully complete three touches (any combination of behind-knee touches or forcing hands to mat)

### 1.2 - [Single Leg Takedown Variations](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/standing/single-leg-takedown-variations.md)

**Overview:** A progressive drilling game where partners work through three different single leg positions, alternating roles as they achieve success in each position

**Time:** 6 minutes, 2 partners or 12 minutes total - switching back and forth

#### Position Progression
Players start at Position 1 and progress to the next position after winning. When either player wins any position, switch roles.

- **Position 1:** Standard single leg grip - opponent's leg held with hands and arms between your legs, head on chest  
- **Position 2:** Ankle grip control on defender's leg  
- **Position 3:** Defender is in turtle position

#### Attacking Player
**Win Condition:**
- Positions 1 & 2: Get defender's hips on the ground and cover the hips, OR get defender's hands on the mat and secure a rear bodylock/front headlock
- Position 3: Get defender to a hip and cover the hips

#### Defending Player
**Win Condition:**
- Positions 1 & 2: Get both legs free and face your opponent
- Position 3: Turn and face your opponent with both knees in between you

## Guard Drills

### 2.1 - [HQ Position Passing Drill](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/guard/supine/hq-position-passing-drill.md)

**Overview:** A drill focusing on the Headquarters (HQ) passing position, modified so the top player starts to the side of both of the bottom player's legs rather than splitting them

**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

#### Top Player
**Position:** Standing to the side of your partner's legs, not splitting them  
**Objective:** Maintain connections to the knee line and head over head in an effort to keep them pinned on their back, either outside both legs or splitting them. Use hands and movement to keep your partner's connections off of you, breaking grips and keeping feet off of you  
**Constraints:** Stay as close to your partner as possible, try not to disconnect. Stay standing

#### Bottom Player
**Position:** Lying on your back with both legs together to one side, your partner standing outside both legs  
**Objective:** Keep your feet and hands between you and your partner.  
**Win:** Make your partner's hips touch the mat

## Pinning Drills

### 3.1 - [Side Control Framing and Pinning](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/pinning/side-control-framing-and-pinning.md)

**Overview:** A pinning drill where the bottom player works to maintain frames at the hip and neck to prevent control, modified so the top player starts with a body lock around the bottom player's waist

**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

#### Bottom Player
**Position:** On your back, framing at the hip and neck, with your partner's body lock already secured around your waist  
**Objective:** Prevent the top player from establishing a crossface and underhook control  
**Win:** Get your legs back in between you and your partner or successfully get on top

#### Top Player
**Position:** On top, in front of your partner's legs, with a body lock secured around their waist  
**Objective:** Stay on top, keep the bottom player pinned, and establish a crossface with underhook control  
**Win:** Successfully maintain the pin and establish a crossface with underhook control

### 3.2 - [Progressive Mount Wrist Control to Isolation](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/pinning/mount/progressive-mount-wrist-control-to-isolation.md)

**Overview:** A progressive drilling game focusing on establishing wrist control from mount position and using it to isolate both of the bottom player's arms in preparation for submissions

**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

#### Position Progression
Players start at Position 1 and progress to the next position after winning. When either player wins any position, switch roles.

#### Attacking Player
**Objective:** Get under both arms and isolate them bicep to ear  
**Win Condition:** Successfully isolate both arms bicep to ear for 3 seconds

**Starting Positions:**
- Position 1: Mount with 1 wrist pinned to the ground
- Position 2: Mount with wrist grips on chest
- Position 3: Side control

#### Defending Player
**Objective:** Get both your knees and hands in front of your opponent  
**Win Condition:** Both knees and both hands positioned in front of opponent (defensive frames on hips with knees between you and opponent)

### 3.3 - [Back Control Any Attack](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/pinning/back-control-any-attack.md)

**Overview:** An advanced back control drill where the attacking partner can use any legal submission from back control, modified to start mid-escape with the defending player turned out to their side

**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

#### Attacking Player
**Position:** Back control with both hooks in and a figure four grip on the defending player's arm, defending player turned out to their side, your choking arm positioned along their side between you rather than around the neck  
**Objective:** Use any legal method to achieve submission from back control  
**Win:** Successfully finish any submission starting from back control

#### Defending Player
**Position:** Being controlled from the back with both hooks in and a figure four grip on one arm, turned out to your side with your near-side elbow framed between yourself and the attacker, shoulders on the mat, and the attacker's choking arm positioned along your side between you  
**Objective:** Escape back control and improve position  
**Win:** Successfully escape back control before submission is achieved

## Conclusion
Finish class with Q&A and some live rolling starting from back control with both hooks in. Focus on applying the passing, pinning, and back control concepts learned during class, as well as practicing the takedown entries and escape framing when opportunities arise.
