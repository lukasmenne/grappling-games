# Upper Body Attacks from Guard Class
**Class Length:** 1 hour

This class focuses on upper body submissions from guard - triangles, armbars, kimuras, and omoplatas. The class opens standing with a progressive single leg series and a guard pulling battle, then moves to the ground where students learn to attack the arms and neck from open guard with the feet on the hips and from collar sleeve guard with a lazy lasso. Two demonstration and drilling sections cover the omoplata as both a submission and a sweep.

This class consists of several progressive drills that build upon each other. Each drill is available as an individual game file for reference.

1. [Single Leg Takedown Variations](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/standing/single-leg-takedown-variations.md)
2. [Guard Pull to Pass](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/guard/guard-pull-to-pass.md)

## Standing Drills

### 1.1 - [Single Leg Takedown Variations](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/standing/single-leg-takedown-variations.md)

**Overview:** A progressive drilling game where partners work through three different single leg positions, alternating roles as they achieve success in each position.

**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

**Position Progression:**
Players start at Position 1 and progress to the next position after winning. When either player wins any position, switch roles.

- **Position 1:** Standard single leg grip - opponent's leg held with hands and arms between your legs, head on chest  
- **Position 2:** Ankle grip control on defender's leg  
- **Position 3:** Defender is in turtle position  

#### Attacking Player
**Win Condition:**
- Positions 1 & 2: Get defender's hips on the ground and cover the hips, OR get defender's hands on the mat and secure a rear bodylock
- Position 3: Get defender to a hip and cover the hips

#### Defending Player
**Win Condition:**
- Positions 1 & 2: Get both legs free and face your opponent
- Position 3: Turn and face your opponent

### 1.2 - [Guard Pull to Pass](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/guard/guard-pull-to-pass.md)

**Overview:** A guard pulling drill where one player pulls guard with collar and sleeve grips while the other player works to immediately remove the guard puller's feet and pass without going to their knees.

**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

#### Guard Puller
**Position:** Standing with collar and sleeve grip on your partner  
**Objective:** Pull guard and off-balance your partner to prevent them from passing your guard  
**Win:** Successfully off-balance your partner (forcing them to both hands or butt), maintain guard, or stand up

#### Top Player
**Position:** Standing, being gripped by your partner  
**Objective:** Remove the guard puller's feet as quickly as possible after they hit the ground and pass the guard  
**Win:** Successfully pass the guard and achieve chest to chest or chest to back contact

### 2.2 - [Closed Guard to Feet on Hips - Upper Body Attacks](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/guard/closed/closed-guard-feet-on-hips-upper-body-attacks.md)

**Overview:** A guard drill starting from closed guard where the guard player opens to both feet on the hips with grips on both sleeves, attacking with triangles, armbars, kimuras, and omoplatas, while the top player works to clear the feet and grips and pass.

**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

#### Guard Player
**Position:** On your back starting in closed guard, then opening your guard to put both feet on your partner's hips with a grip on each of their sleeves  
**Objective:** Use your feet and sleeve grips to off-balance your partner and attack with a triangle, armbar, kimura, or omoplata, or sweep to the top position  
**Win:** Successfully lock up or finish a triangle, armbar, kimura, or omoplata (depending on comfortability), or sweep and come on top

#### Top Player
**Position:** Kneeling inside your partner's guard with both of their feet on your hips and both of your sleeves gripped  
**Objective:** Strip the sleeve grips, clear the feet off your hips, and pass the guard  
**Win:** Successfully pass the guard and establish chest-to-chest or chest-to-back contact with your hands connected

### 2.4 - [Collar Sleeve Lazy Lasso vs Standing Pass](https://lukasmenne.github.io/grappling-games/md-viewer.html?file=games/guard/supine/collar-sleeve-lazy-lasso-vs-standing.md)

**Overview:** A guard drill where the guard player starts with collar and sleeve grips, a lazy lasso hook, and a foot on the opposite bicep against a standing passer, attacking with omoplatas, triangles, armbars, and kimuras while the top player works to clear the lasso and pass.

**Time:** 3 minutes, 2 partners or 6 minutes total - switching back and forth

#### Guard Player
**Position:** On your back with a collar grip and a sleeve grip, a lazy lasso hook over the sleeve-controlled arm (shin over the arm, foot not fully wrapped underneath), and your other foot on the bicep of your partner's collar-side arm, with your partner standing  
**Objective:** Use the lasso and grips to break your partner's posture and off-balance them, attacking with an omoplata, triangle, armbar, or kimura, or sweeping to the top position  
**Win:** Successfully lock up or finish an omoplata, triangle, armbar, or kimura (depending on comfortability), or sweep and come on top

#### Top Player
**Position:** Standing inside your partner's guard with your collar and sleeve controlled, one arm caught in a lazy lasso hook, and a foot on your other bicep  
**Objective:** Strip the grips, clear the lasso hook and the foot on your bicep, and pass the guard  
**Win:** Successfully pass the guard and establish chest-to-chest or chest-to-back contact with your hands connected

## Conclusion
Finish class with Q&A and some live rolling starting from closed guard or collar sleeve guard. Focus on applying the upper body attacks learned during class - triangles, armbars, kimuras, and omoplatas - and on using the omoplata as a sweep when the submission is not available.
